One-time setup

Kokoro Voice needs to download its voices and speech recognition before first use — about 500 MB. It only happens once, and everything is stored on this computer.

Nothing leaves this computer. The voices and the speech recognition run locally. There is no account and no API key, and once installed the engine makes no network calls at all.

What it does

Read aloud

⌃⌘

Select text in any app and press the key. Press again with something new selected to switch to it; press with nothing new selected to pause and resume.

Dictate

⇧⌘

Hold the key, speak, then let go. What you said is typed into whatever you were working in, and also copied to the clipboard as a backup — that copy is cleared again after 45 seconds so it doesn't linger.

Needs Microphone permission.

Snip & read

F7

Press the key and drag a box around anything on screen — it gets read to you. This is for text you can't select: pictures, PDFs, video frames, screenshots someone sent you. The screenshot is deleted the moment the words are pulled out of it.

Needs Screen Recording permission.

Voice & speed

Permissions

macOS asks before any app can see what you have selected, hear your microphone, or read your screen. You'll be prompted the first time you use each feature. To change an answer later:

  • Accessibility — read the selected text, type dictation
  • Microphone — dictation
  • Screen Recording — snip & read
